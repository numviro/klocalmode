HOW WE HANDLE TRENDS

Freshness matters.

A trending page can become misleading much faster than a subway or palace guide.

01

Show the update date

Readers should immediately know when we last checked the information.

02

Remove dead trends

Expired pop-ups and old viral products shouldn't pretend to be current.

03

Connect trends to evergreen guides

A trending Seongsu pop-up can link to a permanent Seongsu travel guide later.

K Local Mode Trending Rule

This section should answer:

What's actually popular now?
Is it still open?
Is the hype mostly online or are Koreans actually going?
Where is it?
Do I need a reservation?
How long will it be available?
Is it worth adding to a short Korea trip?

We won't keep an old trend page labeled “right now” after the trend is gone.
