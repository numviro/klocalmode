REAL EXAMPLES

Textbook Korean vs useful Korean.

We want to explain not only what a sentence means, but whether Koreans would actually use it in that situation.

이거 주세요
igeo juseyo
“This one, please.” Simple and genuinely useful while shopping or ordering.
안 매운 거 있어요?
an maeun geo isseoyo?
“Do you have anything that's not spicy?” Very practical for travelers.
여기 맞아요?
yeogi majayo?
“Is this the right place?” Useful when checking a destination.
ㅋㅋㅋ
kkk / Korean texting laughter
Not a word you pronounce in conversation. It's a texting expression that needs cultural context.
