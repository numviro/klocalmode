Where to Stay in Seoul for First-Time Visitors

Myeongdong, Hongdae, Jongno, Gangnam or somewhere else? The best Seoul neighborhood depends on what you want to do, how late you stay out and how much time you want to spend commuting.

Updated August 2026

The quick answer

Best overall for most first-time visitors:
Myeongdong or nearby central Seoul.

Best for nightlife and younger travelers:
Hongdae.

Best for palaces and traditional Seoul:
Jongno / Insadong.

Best for modern Seoul and south-of-the-river plans:
Gangnam.

Best for KTX and easy intercity travel:
Seoul Station.

Best for Lotte World and southeast Seoul:
Jamsil.

The most important rule: choose the area before the hotel

Seoul is a huge city. A hotel can look excellent on a booking site, but if it puts you far from the places you want to visit, you may spend a surprising amount of your trip on the subway.

For a first trip, I would choose the neighborhood first and the hotel second.

Local Mode Tip:
Don't ask only “Is this hotel in Seoul?”

Ask:

• Which subway station is closest?
• Which subway lines are there?
• How far is the hotel from the station exit?
• Is the walk uphill?
• Is the area still convenient late at night?
• How difficult will the airport transfer be with luggage?

1. Myeongdong — best all-around choice for most first-timers

BEST OVERALL FOR FIRST TRIP

Myeongdong

Shopping · Central location · Tourist infrastructure

If someone asked me where to stay on a first trip to Seoul without giving me any other information, Myeongdong would be one of the first areas I'd check.

It's centrally located for many classic Seoul attractions and is one of the city's best-known shopping districts.

Why it works Central for many first-trip sights
Large choice of hotels
Shopping and food nearby
Tourist services are easy to find
What to know Busy and touristy
Not the best local neighborhood feel
Some restaurants and stores target tourists heavily

Myeongdong is especially useful if your first trip includes places such as Gyeongbokgung, Insadong, Namdaemun, N Seoul Tower, Dongdaemun and central Seoul.

The official Visit Seoul guide also treats Myeongdong as one of the city's major visitor areas and highlights it as a major shopping district.

Check the station, not just “Myeongdong.”
Hotels marketed as being in Myeongdong can be closer to Myeong-dong Station, Euljiro 1-ga or other nearby stations. That can change your daily route considerably.

2. Hongdae — best for nightlife, cafés and younger travelers

Hongdae

Nightlife · Cafés · Music · Airport access

Hongdae has a very different atmosphere from Myeongdong.

It's a university area known for music, performances, cafés, bars, shopping and late-night activity. The official Seoul tourism site also describes Hongdae as a center of pop and indie culture, especially at night.

Why it works Strong nightlife
Lots of cafés and restaurants
Younger atmosphere
Hongik University Station connects to AREX
What to know Can be loud on weekends
Farther from some historic sights
Large area — hotel location matters

Hongdae is a particularly strong choice if you expect to stay out late and want cafés, bars and activity around your hotel.

Airport advantage:
The AREX All-Stop Train serves Hongik University Station, so Hongdae can be convenient for travelers arriving from Incheon Airport.

3. Jongno / Insadong — best for traditional Seoul

Jongno / Insadong

Palaces · Hanok · Traditional culture · Central Seoul

Choose this area if the historic side of Seoul is one of the main reasons you're visiting.

Insadong is known for traditional shops, tea houses, galleries and cultural streets, while the surrounding Jongno area puts you close to palaces and older parts of central Seoul.

Why it works Close to palaces
Traditional atmosphere
Very central for sightseeing
Good for slower cultural trips
What to know Quieter nightlife
Less of the modern shopping atmosphere of Hongdae or Gangnam

The official Seoul tourism guide describes Insadong as a central neighborhood known for antique shops, folk art, traditional tea houses and galleries.

4. Gangnam — best if your plans are mostly south of the Han River

Gangnam

Modern Seoul · Shopping · Business · Nightlife

Gangnam is famous internationally, but that doesn't automatically make it the best base for a first Seoul trip.

It works very well when your plans are concentrated around southern Seoul — for example Gangnam Station, COEX, Apgujeong, Cheongdam or nearby areas.

Why it works Modern Seoul experience
Shopping and restaurants
Business hotels
Nightlife and K-culture nearby
What to know Longer trips to historic central Seoul
Can mean crossing the river repeatedly
Not my default pick for a classic first-time itinerary
Common first-trip mistake:
Booking Gangnam simply because the name is famous, then realizing most of your sightseeing is around Gyeongbokgung, Insadong, Myeongdong and central Seoul.

5. Seoul Station — best for transportation convenience

Seoul Station

AREX · KTX · Intercity travel

Seoul Station can be extremely practical if transportation is more important to you than neighborhood atmosphere.

It connects with AREX and major rail services, which is useful if you're arriving from Incheon Airport or planning trips to other Korean cities.

Why it works AREX access
KTX access
Central transportation hub
Good for multi-city Korea trips
What to know Less interesting immediately around some hotels
Large station can involve long walks with luggage

6. Jamsil — best for Lotte World and family trips

Jamsil

Lotte World · Families · Concerts · Southeast Seoul

Jamsil makes sense when major parts of your itinerary are already in southeast Seoul.

It's especially convenient for Lotte World, Lotte World Tower and events around the Jamsil area.

Why it works Excellent for Lotte World
Family-friendly attractions
Modern shopping and dining
Useful for nearby concerts and events
What to know Far from many historic attractions
Not the most efficient base for a classic central-Seoul itinerary

Which area should you choose?

It's your first trip and you want the easiest all-around location Myeongdong / central Seoul.
You want nightlife, cafés and a younger atmosphere Hongdae.
You care most about palaces, traditional streets and history Jongno / Insadong.
Your itinerary is mostly Gangnam, COEX and southern Seoul Gangnam.
You're taking KTX to other cities Consider Seoul Station.
Your trip revolves around Lotte World or Jamsil events Jamsil.

My choice for a typical 3–5 day first trip

For a typical first visit that mixes palaces, Myeongdong, traditional neighborhoods, markets, shopping and a few modern Seoul areas, I'd usually start my hotel search around Myeongdong or nearby central Seoul.

That doesn't mean Myeongdong is objectively the “best” neighborhood. It means it tends to create fewer transportation compromises for a common first-time itinerary.

If nightlife matters more than historic sightseeing, I'd move Hongdae much higher.

Don't book until you check these 6 things

1. Nearest station Which subway station is actually closest?
2. Station exit How far is the hotel from the correct exit?
3. Airport route How will you get there with luggage?
4. Your daily itinerary Are most of your plans north or south of the Han River?
5. Nighttime Do you want nightlife near your hotel or a quieter area?
6. Walking conditions Check hills, stairs and the final walk with luggage.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best area to stay in Seoul for a first-time visitor?

For many first-time visitors, Myeongdong or another convenient central Seoul area is a strong all-around choice. However, Hongdae, Jongno and Gangnam can be better for specific travel styles.

Is Myeongdong a good place to stay?

Yes, especially for a first trip focused on central sightseeing, shopping and convenient tourist infrastructure.

Is Hongdae better than Myeongdong?

It depends on your priorities. Hongdae is generally stronger for nightlife and cafés, while Myeongdong is usually more convenient for a classic central-Seoul sightseeing itinerary.

Should I stay in Gangnam on my first trip?

Gangnam is a good choice if much of your itinerary is south of the Han River. If most of your plans involve palaces and historic central Seoul, another area may reduce travel time.

Where should families stay in Seoul?

It depends on the itinerary. Jamsil is convenient for Lotte World, while central Seoul may work better for families planning a broader first-time sightseeing trip.
